# Showcase images

Screenshots for the auto-scrolling gallery on the purchase screen.

Drop them in here as PNG or JPEG and say so — the gallery still renders lettered
placeholders until it is pointed at real files.

**Take them from the home-screen app, not from Safari.** Add to Home Screen
first, then screenshot: no address bar, no toolbar, and the real safe-area
layout. A Safari screenshot shows chrome that will never appear in the shipped
app.

Native resolution is right (iPhone 12 is 1170x2532). The strip draws each shot
at 104pt wide on a 390:844 frame, so anything at that ratio drops in without the
strip changing height, and anything above ~780px wide is already past what a 3x
screen can show. They are converted and resized on the way into the build; do
not pre-shrink them.

Six slots, currently standing in for: Plain, Dark, Light, Needle, Strobe,
Tunings. Any number works — the loop measures itself.
