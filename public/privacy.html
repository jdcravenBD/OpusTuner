Privacy Policy

Nothing is collected.
Nothing is sent.

Last updated 29 August 2026

Easy as Tuning collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

The microphone

The app listens through your device's microphone in order to work out the pitch of the note you are playing. That is the entire purpose of the permission, and it is the only thing the audio is used for.

  • Audio is analysed on your device, as it arrives, and then discarded.
  • No recording is made. Nothing is written to storage.
  • No audio, and nothing derived from audio, is sent off the device.
  • The microphone is released whenever the app is in the background, and whenever you switch the tuner off.

iOS will not grant microphone access without asking you first, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Microphone. The app will simply stop hearing anything.

What is stored on your device

Your settings are saved on your device so the app opens the way you left it: reference pitch, chosen tuning, appearance, and any custom tunings you build. They stay there, are included in your own device backups if you make them, and are removed when you delete the app. They are not readable by anyone else and are never uploaded.

Purchases

Easy as Tuning offers a single one-time purchase. It is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; payment details are never seen by, or shared with, this app or its developer. What the app receives back from Apple is confirmation that the purchase exists, and nothing else.

There is no subscription. Nothing renews and nothing expires.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. Since it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be updated on this page, with the date at the top revised. A change that affected what the app does with your data would also be described in the App Store release notes.
