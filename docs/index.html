The tuner is not picking up my instrument

Check that the microphone permission was allowed. On iOS that lives in Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Microphone. If it is on and the needle still sits still, open Settings inside the app and raise Sensitivity. A quiet instrument, an unplugged electric guitar in particular, sits a long way below an acoustic and needs the gate opened up.

The reading jumps around while a note fades

Damp the strings you are not tuning. Two strings ringing together are genuinely periodic at a note neither of them is playing, and every tuner ever made has to guess in that situation. Turning off Auto and picking the string yourself also helps on a resonant instrument.

I paid, and it is asking me to pay again

Open the purchase screen and tap Restore underneath the price. That asks Apple what your account already owns and unlocks it again. It works on any device signed in to the same Apple ID, and it is free.

If nothing comes back, check you are signed in to the Apple ID you bought it with. Purchases belong to the account, not the handset.

What is free, and what is not

Every instrument's own standard tuning is free, along with both tuner displays and one custom tuning of your own. If you own a seven-string guitar or a five-string bass, the app works for you without paying anything.

The Full Set is a single payment and adds the chromatic tuner, the alternate tunings, drops, opens, modal and cross tunings, unlimited custom tunings, the colour themes, and a switch to hide the wordmark. It is not a subscription and it never becomes one.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It works completely offline. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere. See the privacy policy for the detail.
